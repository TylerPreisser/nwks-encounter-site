When your human partner names a couple of specific things ("my orchestration skill and the testing
skill"), don't latch onto only those literal items. Use judgment and consider the whole system they
are part of, then propose/include it.

**Why:** Asked to package "the orchestration files," a narrow reading grabbed only the two named
skills. The push-back: "Why are you only thinking about the two things I said instead of using your
own intelligence? The orchestration includes many things — it includes the testing." The real
apparatus was the orchestration skill + the testing skill + the coordination config they depend on.

**How to apply:** Map out the full set of related artifacts before acting, present the comprehensive
interpretation (with dependencies/companions surfaced), and let your partner trim — rather than
starting narrow and making them expand the scope. Related: [[working-style-respect-time-and-trust]].
